# SkinManager 1.1.4

**Release date: 2026-11-14**

SkinManager 1.1.4 adds animated backgrounds, more flexible color editing, and clearer settings for the Codex desktop app on Windows. Use GIF backgrounds, fine-tune colors, choose what starts when you sign in to Windows, and select SkinManager's interface language.

## What's new

- Preserve GIF animation when importing, editing, previewing, and exporting skins. Market covers stay static, while downloaded skins retain their animation.
- Adjust colors with a hue ring and saturation/brightness area, with HEX, RGB, and alpha support. Preview changes live, then confirm or cancel them.
- Settings are grouped under General, Appearance, and Compatibility. Choose System, Simplified Chinese, or English for SkinManager; it does not change Codex's interface language.
- Choose whether SkinManager starts when you sign in to Windows and whether it starts the Codex debug build and applies the last selected skin.
- Sign in with a market account and choose Suggestions → Feedback in the app, or Feedback beside My submissions on the website. Provide a title and message. Emails include account details and the submission source; app submissions also include the SkinManager version. Replies go to the account email. Attachments are not supported.
- Fine-editor previews update more smoothly during continuous adjustments and show the final value when the interaction ends.

## Installation and compatibility

- Supports Windows x64 and the Microsoft Store version of the Codex desktop app.
- Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime is required for the interface preview and market account sign-in.
- Skins are not included in the installer. Download them from the market or import your own.
- Install over an existing version to keep the skin library and preferences. Reapply the skin after upgrading.
- The installer is not code-signed.
